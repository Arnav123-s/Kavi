This is source-derived quantitative teaching. The teaching layer supplies semantic quantity roles, assumptions, SI conversions and numerical examples. The learner infers executable arrangements from those examples. It does not receive a formula string as its learned program, but the grammar, quantity bindings and scientific interpretation are supplied. Reading or retaining a source document in the local archive does not mean that the model has acquired its language. No original prose or textbook chapter is copied into the deployed model.

Keep local source bodies and retrieval failures in the ignored source folder. A local download of the Tübingen scan failed certificate verification; its text was inspected through the research tool, and no security check was disabled. The original papers span 1822–1926 and three languages. This is a first selection, not coverage of scientific history or world languages.

## Finite course

Use one visible worker, five minutes including pauses, a 512 MiB observed worker limit and at most five million counted units per fit. Reuse the readable display and Pause/Resume/Stop controls. Record all candidate work, temporary evidence and persistent bytes. Maximum two teaching rounds precede the final freeze.

Acquire nine scalar configuration families: sum, difference, product/work, quotient, thermal energy, inverse thermal relation, rest energy, mass–energy conversion and identity. The engine connects them using twelve explicit quantity relations. Only the dependency paths needed for the requested quantity execute. Transferring all work to heat requires an explicit full-thermalization condition.

Each fit enumerates zero-, one- and two-call graphs over four supplied real arithmetic operations. Two-input fits contain 594 candidates; three-input fits contain 2,343; one-input fits contain 69. Count every candidate. The earlier acquired natural-number arithmetic remains a separate dependency; the real arithmetic adapters are supplied host operations.

Begin with four examples for basic arithmetic/identity and one deliberately ambiguous example for each of the four scientific formula families. Record the first textbook practice results, including mistakes. Then provide sixteen varied, source-derived numerical examples per family, using seed 95100 plus its fixed family index. These clarify proportionality and which quantities divide or multiply. Repeat the practice set; if a quantitative mistake remains, add its verified intermediate correction to that family's teaching evidence and fit once more. Failures remaining after that finite correction round are reported.

Practice uses OpenStax *University Physics*, by Samuel J. Ling, Jeff Sanny and William Moebs: [volume 2, chapter 1, problems 61, 62, 63(a–d), 64 and the numerical part of 65](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-2/pages/1-problems); [volume 3, chapter 5, problems 59 and 60 in joules and MeV, and 63(a)](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-3/pages/5-problems). This yields thirteen numerical targets. It excludes material identification and other qualitative subparts. The questions are supplied as annotated quantities and requested outputs. The model is not credited with parsing the textbook prose.

Use the values in the question and its supplied property table. Record all unit conversions and added constants. Practice examples can influence learning; their later success is not a held-out result. OpenStax retains authorship of its questions. Public results contain identifiers and numerical outcomes rather than chapter text.

## Retention, sharing and freeze

After corrections, check 128 synthetic inputs per scalar family using seed 95200 plus the family index. These inputs assess transfer within the declared formula grammar. They do not constitute a graduate examination. Check earlier composed arithmetic and physical evolution paths, then share only exactly identical configuration definitions. Repeat the same checks after sharing.

Save the configuration and its SHA-256 fingerprint. End the training worker before retrieving the examination questions. Any examination worker must load that exact artifact, perform no fitting and verify that its bytes are unchanged afterwards.
